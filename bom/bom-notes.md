# BOM notes

- Costs are indicative USD prices at quantity 1 for a concept estimate, not quotes. Every line is priced. Value-engineering target: USD 150 (`budget_usd`, a hypothetical control target). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 139.50 for the base node (USD 10.50 under the target); USD 148.50 with the sun shield, line 14 (USD 1.50 under the target) (FND-CAL-001, section F).
- This is the cost of the FieldNode core (enclosure, power, radio, panel and mounting). Sibling projects that use FieldNode cite this figure when they cost their own parts separately. Sensors and the gateway are not included (see TwinKit).
- Line numbers 1 to 12 and 15 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png` and the part keys in `cad/src/model.py`. Line 13 has no callout. Line 14 is modeled by `build_shield()` in `cad/src/model.py` and exported as `cad/step/fieldnode-shield.step`; it is not shown in the media.
- TRL 3 changes: line 3 now has both glands fitted (one carries the panel lead), with the blanking plug moved to line 13; line 11 is printed ASA rather than aluminium, which saves about 0.11 kg.
- Design for construction (FND-DDR-003, 2026-09-30) repriced lines 1, 5, 13 and 14 and added line 15, the plug-in connectors and rail fuses.
- The power board (line 7) is priced as a prototype carrier; its design is TRL 4 work and has not been started. It now carries the serial programming header (1 x 6 pins at 2.54 mm: ground, 3.3 V, TX, RX, boot, reset), wired to the controller and facing the lid, so firmware is updated by cable inside the box with the lid open and no extra hole in the enclosure (FND-DEC-001, 2026-10-02). The header adds USD 0.50 (distributor price class at quantity 1), so line 7 is now USD 22.50. The STM32WL-class module has no USB port, so a serial header is the simpler choice; the 3.3 V USB-to-serial cable is a tool, not a part of the node.
- The antenna (line 9) is a 915 MHz whip for the US915 default first variant (Amish, 2026-10-02, FND-DEC-001), at the same price; it changes to the band of the first adopting project's site if that is outside North America.
- The sensor ports (line 10) are wired to the proposed standard pinout: pin 1 switched rail, pin 2 data A, pin 3 ground, pin 4 data B, pin 5 analog (FND-DEC-001).
- Changes from Amish's 2026-09-25 decisions (FND-DDR-002): line 4 is now a 9 V class panel of the same power, at the same indicative price; line 14, a ventilated white aluminium sun shield (USD 9.00, 0.16 kg with its thumb screws), is an option for sites whose design maximum exceeds 30 °C.
